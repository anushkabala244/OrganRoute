ABOUT ORGANROUTE

Making the Journey Easier to Navigate

Finding the right healthcare information can feel overwhelming. OrganRoute aims to make the process easier to understand by bringing support, hospital information and request tracking together in one place.

Our goal is to help people navigate authorised transplant-related services while respecting privacy, verification and the importance of medical professionals.

✦

Built Around People

Separate experiences for patients, families, hospital staff and administrators.

Designed for Trust

Verification, protected information and clear request statuses are part of the plan.
